OffChat

Chat with people nearby without internet, over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi.

Why we built it

Networks fail exactly when lots of people are together: festivals, concerts, flights, cruises, protests, power cuts. Every messenger assumes a connection and an account. OffChat assumes neither.

Phones talk directly to each other, and messages hop through other nearby phones to reach further. There are no OffChat servers, so your messages only ever live on the phones you chat with.

What's inside

  • Channels and direct messages, no SIM or sign-up
  • End-to-end encrypted DMs, every message signed
  • Mesh relay and store-and-forward
  • Photos, voice, replies, reactions
  • Android and iPhone can chat with each other

Sitspark

Calm, reward-based dog training in short daily sessions.

Why we built it

Most owners don't need a theory course. They need the next five minutes of training, done right, every day. Sitspark turns force-free methods into short guided sessions with a step player that reads each step aloud.

The private AI trainer runs on the phone with Gemini Nano, so questions about your dog never leave the device. No account, no server.

What's inside

  • 9 classes, 46 lessons, puppies to adult dogs
  • Clicker, session timer, potty log, bark monitor
  • Puppy socialization tracker and breed tips
  • On-device AI trainer and 7-day behaviour plans
  • 12 languages; first class free

TripSpeak

Speak the language of your trip: ready phrases and a daily plan.

Why we built it

Travellers don't need grammar tables, they need to order a coffee and ask for directions in three weeks' time. TripSpeak teaches the phrases for your trip on a plan that adapts to your travel date.

It started as TravelFrench and grew into one app with 12 courses. Existing TravelFrench users keep their progress and subscription.

What's inside

  • 12 languages, from Italian to Japanese and Thai
  • Listen, read, write, speak, with pronunciation feedback
  • Smart review and an offline phrasebook
  • Camera translator for menus and signs
  • Private on-device AI conversation practice
