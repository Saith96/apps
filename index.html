OffChat: Find Your Friends

Find your friends and message them nearby, with no internet at all.

Visit website →

Why we built it

Networks fail exactly when lots of people are together: festivals, concerts, stadiums, ski slopes, cruises and planes. That is also where you lose each other in the crowd. OffChat does both without a connection: it points you to your friends and carries your messages.

Phones talk directly to each other over Bluetooth, and messages hop through other nearby phones to reach further. There are no OffChat servers and no accounts, so your messages and your direction only ever reach the people you share them with. It's free to use and now speaks 52 languages; OffChat Plus is a single purchase, never a subscription.

What's inside

  • An arrow and the distance to a friend who lets you find them
  • Ask their screen to light up so you can spot them in a crowd
  • Private groups, channels and encrypted DMs, no SIM or sign-up
  • Mesh relay, store-and-forward, photos, voice and reactions
  • Save the meeting point or the car and walk back with an arrow

Singlark: Voice Coach

A private voice coach: know your voice in two minutes, then watch it improve.

Visit website →

Why we built it

Most people who sing for fun never get feedback on their voice. Lessons are expensive, and karaoke apps score the song, not the singer. Singlark measures your voice in a two-minute check-up, then builds a five-minute daily workout around your range and your weakest score.

Your voice is personal, so it stays on your phone. Pitch detection runs on the device: nothing is uploaded, with no account and no ads.

What's inside

  • Two-minute check-up: range, voice type, accuracy, steadiness
  • Live pitch line and a tuning needle for free practice
  • A 5-minute daily workout planned around your range
  • Learn a song phrase by phrase, plus ear training and harmony
  • Check-up clips so you can hear your progress
